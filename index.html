Old School Bitcoin

One CPU, one vote.

OSBTC is a fork of Bitcoin Core with Bitcoin's original 2009 emission and a CPU-friendly proof of work, so ordinary computers can mine it.

Status: private testnet. There is no public network, no coins for sale, and no launch date yet.

Numbers

Max supply21,000,000 OSBTC
Block reward50 OSBTC, halving every 210,000 blocks
Block time10 minutes
Difficulty retargetEvery 2 weeks, as Bitcoin
Coinbase maturity100 blocks
PremineNone

Proof of work: OSHash-Y

Header → SHA-256d → yespower (2 MiB memory) → SHA3-256 → compared to the target. The memory-hard step favours CPUs over ASICs.
